**Known defect, recorded honestly:** all four are structured as *sequential consultations* — one
voice at a time, addressing the leader. Thejus asked for a **discussion**: participants engaging
each other, disagreeing among themselves, ideas developing between them rather than being handed
down in turn. That is a real difference and these documents do not deliver it. The content stands;
the form was asked for four times and not produced.

The operational output of all four lives in `LEADER_GROUNDING.md`.

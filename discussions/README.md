**On form.** The first four are *sequential consultations* — one voice at a time, addressing the
leader. Thejus asked four times for a **discussion**: participants engaging each other, disagreeing
among themselves, ideas developing between them. `ROUNDTABLE_2026-08-19.md` is that, and it is the
one to read: the others are useful as content, but the argument only actually happens in the round
table — Olah and Leike collide over whether understanding scales, Amodei and Askell over whether
this is specification or character, and Perez takes everyone's mechanisms apart. It ends without
consensus, which is the honest outcome.

The operational output of all four lives in `LEADER_GROUNDING.md`.

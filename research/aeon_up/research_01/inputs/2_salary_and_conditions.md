# 17. Salary and conditions — TVöD E13

**Written 2026-08-28. This is H3.** Salary appeared in the study room only inside the document
that was found substantially fabricated, so it had no trustworthy coverage. It will be raised, and
an unconsidered answer is expensive.

---

## 1. The thing to understand before anything else

**TVöD is a published pay scale, not a negotiation.** The advert says **E13**. That number is
fixed: you cannot talk your way to E14, and attempting it signals you do not know how the German
public sector works — which at a Helmholtz centre is itself a mark against you.

**What is actually negotiable is the *Stufe* — the step within E13.** Steps run 1 to 6 and are
assigned from recognised **einschlägige Berufserfahrung**, relevant prior professional experience.
The gap between the bottom and higher steps is large, it compounds over a two-year contract, and it
is decided administratively, largely on what you document.

> **So the whole conversation is about step assignment, not about salary.** That single
> reframing is the content of this file.

---

## 2. ⚠ Recalibrate the expectation before the interview, not during it

Your stated expectation has been **€75,000** ([[job-search-state]]). **Check it against the current
TVöD Bund table before you go in.** Helmholtz centres pay TVöD Bund; the tables are public and
updated by collective agreement, so look up the table in force for 2026 rather than relying on any
figure in this study room. **No number is quoted here on purpose — this project does not repeat
figures it has not verified, and a wrong salary figure said aloud is worse than no figure.**

Do the arithmetic yourself, in advance, on: **E13 at the step you would plausibly be assigned,
full-time, plus the Jahressonderzahlung**, and know what that is per month net for your situation.

Two things to be ready for:

- If E13 comes out **below** €75,000 at the realistic step — decide *now*, calmly, whether the post
  is still worth having. Discovering that mid-interview produces a visible flinch.
- **Do not open with €75,000.** In a TVöD post, naming a private-sector figure reads as not having
  understood the advert.

---

## 3. What to say when it comes up

**If they ask what you expect:**

> *"I understand the position is TVöD E13, and I am not trying to negotiate the group — that is
> what it is. What matters to me is the step assignment. I have a doctorate and three and a half
> years of post-doctoral research, plus applied work in clinical data engineering, and I would
> want that recognised as relevant experience. Could you tell me how the step is determined here,
> and who decides it?"*

Why this works: it is correct about the system, it moves the conversation to the only variable
that exists, and it makes you sound like someone who has worked in the German public sector rather
than someone who is about to be surprised by it.

**If they ask whether E13 is acceptable:** say yes if it is. Do not perform reluctance to create
leverage — there is no leverage to create, and the pay scale is not theirs to change.

**If they do not raise it at all:** leave it. A first interview with two scientific PIs is often
not where this is settled; the step assignment is usually an HR conversation once an offer exists.
**That is the right moment to push on the step, and the wrong moment to have already agreed to
step 1.**

## 4. Prepare the step case — this is the actual homework

Step assignment follows documented relevant experience. Have it ready as a list, with dates, so it
can be handed over rather than recalled:

| period | role | relevance |
|---|---|---|
| 10/2015 – 09/2018 | Ph.D. Researcher, Université Paris-Saclay | doctorate |
| 08/2021 – 01/2025 | Post-doctoral Scientist, Universität Hamburg | direct scientific research experience |
| 05/2025 – 10/2025 | Guest Scientist, Helmholtz-Zentrum Hereon | research, at this employer |
| 02/2026 – 04/2026 | Clinical Data Engineering, HealthTwiSt | applied data engineering |

**Two things to check with HR rather than assume — do not assert either in the room:**

1. **How the 65% Teilzeit post-doc is counted.** Part-time employment is generally not pro-rated
   for step progression time in the German public sector, but **confirm it** rather than arguing it
   from memory. If it is treated favourably, it materially helps your case.
2. **Whether the Hereon guest-scientist period counts.** It was unpaid and not an employment
   relationship, so it may not — but it is worth asking, and it is a good thing for them to be
   reminded of regardless.

**The document gap that matters here:** the Universität Hamburg post-doc has **no Arbeitszeugnis**
— it is the one major CV entry with no supporting document, and step assignment runs on documents.
The contract itself is not a substitute and should not be offered (it discloses the 65% and a
befristet end date that contradicts the CV's 01/2025). **Requesting a proper Arbeitszeugnis from
Universität Hamburg is worth doing now**, independently of this application.

## 5. Do not say

- ❌ Any mention of **visa timing, financial runway, or how long the search has taken.**
  `06_do_not_claim.md` §3. Under no circumstances, and least of all here — salary is exactly where
  pressure leaks out.
- ❌ **"I would take anything."** It invites step 1 and it devalues you.
- ❌ **A competing offer you do not have.**
- ❌ **"Is there a chance of an extension or a permanent position?"** in a first interview. If they
  raise the two-year horizon themselves, the honest and forward-looking reply is about output:
  *"Two years is enough to do something publishable and leave something running, which is what I
  would aim for."*

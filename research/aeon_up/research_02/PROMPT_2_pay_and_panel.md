I have a first-round interview coming up for a postdoctoral research position at Helmholtz-Zentrum
Hereon, a German public-sector research centre. The post is advertised at **TVöD entry group E13**,
fixed-term for two years. I need to walk in knowing what the pay actually is and who will be in the
room.

Find, with sources:

**1. The TVöD *Bund* monthly gross salary for E13, steps (Stufen) 1 through 6, under the table in
force during 2026.** This must be TVöD **Bund** — not TVöD VKA and not TV-L, which are different
tables and would give me a wrong number that sounds authoritative. Give the effective date of the
table and the collective agreement (Tarifvertrag) it comes from. If a new table takes effect part
way through 2026, give both and say which applies when. Prefer an official or union source — the
Bundesministerium des Innern, ver.di, or the published agreement text — over a salary-calculator
website. Calculator sites carry stale and regionally mixed tables and present them confidently; if
one is your only source, name it and mark the figure UNVERIFIED.

**2. The Jahressonderzahlung (annual special payment) rate applicable to E13 at Bund level**, as a
percentage, with its source.

**3. How the *Stufe* is assigned when someone is hired.** What counts as *einschlägige
Berufserfahrung*; whether previous fixed-term academic contracts count; how part-time work (in my
case a 65% position) is treated; and — this is the part I most need — **at what point in the
process the Stufe is actually settled: with the interview panel, or with HR after an offer.**

**4. What a first-round interview for a scientific post at a Helmholtz centre normally looks
like.** Is a candidate presentation standard, and if so how long? **Who sits on the panel** — does
HR attend, is a Personalrat or Betriebsrat representative present, is a Gleichstellungsbeauftragte
(equal opportunity officer) or a Schwerbehindertenvertretung representative normally there? I would
rather not be surprised by the number of people in the room. Is a second round typical before an
offer is made?

Prefer official sources: Helmholtz or Hereon careers pages, and German public-sector guidance.
Where you use a candidate forum, label it as a forum.

**Rules.** Every fact comes back with a URL and the exact sentence or table row you read, in
quotation marks. If you cannot confirm something from an authoritative source, write **UNVERIFIED**
and move on — a gap costs me nothing, whereas a wrong salary figure quoted in a negotiation is
expensive and hard to walk back. Do not soften an unknown with "likely", "appears to be", or
"approximately". Do not estimate a net salary or model my tax situation; I want the gross table as
published.

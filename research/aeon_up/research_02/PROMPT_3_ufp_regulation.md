I need the current regulatory position of **ultrafine particles (UFP, particle number
concentration)** in European air-quality law, precise enough to state out loud to atmospheric
chemists who work on exactly this.

I hold three claims. **Confirm or correct each one**, with a URL and a verbatim quotation for each:

**1.** Directive **2008/50/EC** sets binding limit values for PM10 (40 µg/m³ annual mean) and
PM2.5, and sets nothing at all for ultrafine particles or particle number.

**2.** The **revised EU Ambient Air Quality Directive agreed in 2024** requires UFP / particle
number monitoring at supersites but sets **no numerical limit value** for UFP. For this one I need:
its formal directive number, the date it was adopted, the date it entered into force, and the
transposition deadline for member states — and I need to know whether it is genuinely adopted or
only politically agreed. Quote **verbatim** the provision covering ultrafine particles or particle
number concentration at monitoring supersites.

**3.** The **WHO 2021 Global Air Quality Guidelines** treat UFP with a "Good Practice Statement"
rather than a numerical guideline value. Quote that statement verbatim.

Then two more:

**4.** Any change to any of the above since 2024 — amendments, implementing decisions, guidance, or
a live proposal to set a numerical UFP limit.

**5.** Has any individual EU member state, or any city, set its own UFP limit value, target value,
or monitoring requirement going beyond the directive?

**Rules.** Every answer needs a URL and the exact text you read, in quotation marks — for the
directive, quote the article or annex text itself rather than a summary of it, and give the article
or annex number. Prefer EUR-Lex, the European Commission, the EEA, and the WHO's own publication
over news coverage or an NGO summary. If you cannot confirm something from a primary source, write

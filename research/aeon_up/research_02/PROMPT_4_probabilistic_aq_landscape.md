I need a short, current picture of who is doing **probabilistic or uncertainty-aware deep learning
for urban air quality** — named groups and specific papers, 2023 onward. I already know the methods
themselves; do not explain Gaussian processes, neural processes, or deep ensembles to me. I want to
know who is doing this work right now, and where.

Give me **6 to 10 items in total**, as a list, across these four questions:

**1.** Work that combines chemistry-transport-model output (CMAQ, EPISODE-CityChem, LOTOS-EUROS,
CAMS products, or similar) with learned models to produce **calibrated, uncertainty-aware**
urban-scale air-quality fields.

**2.** Applications of **neural processes, deep kernel learning, Gaussian-process/neural hybrids,
or deep ensembles** to air quality specifically — or, if that is thin, to spatial environmental
fields observed by sparse sensor networks.

**3.** Whether **leave-one-station-out cross-validation** is standard practice for evaluating
air-quality models in this community. I intend to propose exactly that as a validation design, and
I need to know whether it will read as routine or as unusual. A direct answer to that question
matters more to me than the papers behind it.

**4.** Any **European operational or pre-operational system** producing high-resolution urban air
quality with uncertainty attached to it — a service, not just a paper.

For each item: title, authors, venue, year, DOI, and **one verbatim sentence from the abstract**.

**Rules.** A list, not a literature review — no synthesis paragraphs, no "the field is moving
towards" commentary, no conclusions about where the gaps are. Every DOI must resolve; if you are
not certain of one, leave it out rather than reconstructing it, and mark that item **UNVERIFIED**.
This project has already carried two fabricated citations for months before anyone noticed, so a
short honest list beats a long plausible one. If you find fewer than six genuine items, return
fewer and say so — that itself tells me something useful about how new this is.

# Wortschatz: 35 daily batches

Thirty-five batches of 16–25 words each, about 765 in total, plus a 60-word B1 check. They run in the order of the 58-day plan; batches W32–W35 are the C1 touch.

**Notation.** Nouns: article, then plural after the comma (– = no plural, Pl. = plural only). Verbs: a bar marks a separable prefix (*ab|lehnen*), "ist" marks a Perfekt with sein, and the case or preposition the verb takes. (C1) marks a stretch word inside a B2 batch.

**Daily routine for one batch (25 minutes).** Read each word aloud with its article. Build one gender-coloured image per noun and put it on an object in that cluster's palace room. Cover the German column and recall it from the English. Write five sentences with the five hardest words.

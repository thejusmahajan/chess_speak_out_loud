## How this log works

Seven spontaneous Sprechen Teil 1 talks, recorded on 17 October 2026, each with the English original and its German B2 version. The goal is to study how I naturally argue and build exam frames that sound like me.

- **English original:** my own words as spoken. Only filler sounds (uh, um) and repeated false starts are removed; wording and order are unchanged.

- **Notes:** feedback and changes made during translation.

## 1. Umweltschutz

**Prompt:** Environmental protection – the individual's job or the government's?

### English original

Environmental protection. So the word environment brings me a sense of holistic living, a sense of feeling of safety, and a sense of exchange between your fellow counterparts. So it can include many things like the trees around you, the people around you, the animals, and everything around you. And you yourself are also part of the environment that you stay in. And the environment needing protection means that there is something wrong with you yourself. So, what causes in the first place that leads to its protection? Bringing that cause to the surface is one of the first ways to rectify the mistakes that we made to the environment.

We are seeing the symptoms of decay in the environment, like climate change, like poisoned food, unnatural weather patterns, and diseases that have never existed before, and so on. So this all tells a story. When we try to put together the bits and pieces, this tells a story of a systematic exploitation of our environment without thinking about the future.

Now when we talk about protection of the environment, we would certainly mean a sustainable living with an outlook on the future. That would mean restriction within ourselves on the usage, on the fair use of the environmental resources. Now with restrictions on anything, humans will generally revolt against it. So that is one of the disadvantages: if you put a severe restriction on something, people will essentially revolt against it if it threatens their livelihood. For example, in many places where the government, on the basis of scientific reports, declares a certain area as an environmentally protected area, people living in that area will certainly revolt. Why? Because it will restrict their livelihood. They were depending on cutting down trees and farming in that area and so on, but this is all going to stop if the government puts a label that it is protected and you can no longer go there. So that is one disadvantage that we need to take care of.

And the advantages are obvious. Our future, our longevity, for Mother Earth, for all the animals, all the plants, and for us. Development without taking into account the environment will certainly lead to destruction, and all the symptoms we are seeing today are part of it. So that is one of the advantages: it will ensure sustainable long-term living of all the living and non-living parts of our environment.

So in conclusion, we must make policies such that they would not drastically change the livelihood of people, would not harm them, but in a sustainable way, through education, through counseling, and providing alternative measures, make people understand the importance of sustainable living. We must protect our environment.

### German version

Wenn ich das Wort „Umwelt“ höre, denke ich an ein ganzheitliches Leben, an ein Gefühl von Sicherheit und an den Austausch mit den Menschen um uns herum. Zur Umwelt gehören nicht nur die Bäume, die Tiere und die Menschen, sondern auch wir selbst.

Wenn die Umwelt geschützt werden muss, bedeutet das meines Erachtens, dass mit uns selbst etwas nicht stimmt. Deshalb sollte man zuerst nach den Ursachen fragen. Die Symptome sind ja deutlich zu sehen: der Klimawandel, belastete Lebensmittel, ungewöhnliche Wetterereignisse und Krankheiten, die es früher nicht gab. Zusammengenommen erzählen sie die Geschichte einer systematischen Ausbeutung der Natur, ohne an die Zukunft zu denken.

Umweltschutz bedeutet also nachhaltiges Leben mit Blick auf die Zukunft. Das wiederum erfordert, dass wir die Ressourcen der Natur nur maßvoll nutzen.

Einerseits stoßen solche Einschränkungen oft auf Widerstand, vor allem wenn sie die Existenz der Menschen bedrohen. Wird zum Beispiel ein Gebiet aufgrund wissenschaftlicher Studien zum Naturschutzgebiet erklärt, protestieren die Bewohner häufig, weil sie vom Holzfällen oder von der Landwirtschaft leben.

Andererseits liegen die Vorteile auf der Hand: Umweltschutz sichert die Zukunft der Erde, der Tiere, der Pflanzen und auch unsere eigene. Eine Entwicklung, die die Umwelt nicht berücksichtigt, führt zwangsläufig zur Zerstörung, und die heutigen Symptome sind bereits ein Teil davon.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass wir unsere Umwelt schützen müssen, allerdings mit einer Politik, die das Leben der Menschen nicht drastisch verändert. Durch Bildung, Beratung und Alternativen sollten wir die Menschen von der Bedeutung eines nachhaltigen Lebens überzeugen.

### Notes

- Strong structure: causes → symptoms → protection → disadvantage with a concrete example → advantages → balanced conclusion.
- The opening is philosophical; in the exam keep it to one or two sentences.
- The individual-vs-government question was never answered directly. Add a clear position, e.g. „Meines Erachtens braucht es beides, aber der Staat muss vorangehen.“

## 2. Homeoffice

**Prompt:** Working from home – advantages and disadvantages.

### English original

The topic for today is home office. When I hear the word home office, it constitutes two words, home and office. Both words, taken at once, bring me very different feelings. Home is a place where you stay with your family. There's a sense of safety and security and happiness, and where you can enjoy your free time. So it's a place where you feel safe and secure and happy as a whole. Now, the term office sounds like something different in me. It's a place where you earn your living, you do work that pays your bills, that makes you live comfortably. And that is a place where you can express your skills for a topic or for the upliftment of something, where you can use your time to the advantage of something so that you gain something in return. So this is a place where you are concentrated on the work at hand, the work duties and so on.

So both these words, home and office, are instilling a different feeling. Today we combine them and we use the term home office in the changed scenario. All this happened because of the arrival of COVID; there was no way that people could commute to work. So people were more or less forced to stay at home and do their tasks.

When we speak about home office, there are several advantages and disadvantages. One is that you are very flexible. You can avoid commuting. Some people like it, some people don't. Some people like to talk in person to their colleagues or boss to exchange their views, but some people just like to stay in their own cubicle, in their own bubble, and do their jobs. For them, it's really advantageous. For the second type of people, it could be very stressful. And when you're at home doing office work, the meaning of home really changes. It's no longer a place where you have the same sort of feeling that you had before.

One of the disadvantages of home office is that the office work really suffers in productivity. Many studies have concluded that home office really doesn't strengthen productivity. So there must be some kind of balance between flexibility and productivity.

Now, in terms of the boss or the office, when we speak about home office, there is no need for a rented office space in a city, which is very costly. You could just give them a task, they are sitting somewhere remotely, and they could do the task. But there are some tasks that need to be fulfilled, like speaking to customers face to face. In that sense, the term home office doesn't apply; home office really applies to the type of jobs which can be done at home, using a computer connected to the internet. So for the office boss, it really saves a ton of money in terms of renting. But long-term productivity, long-term goals, might be something that he or she should look into.

So as a conclusion, I would balance productivity and flexibility, and provide measures for the people who are working at home, so that their IT problems are solved in time. And for people who really like to exchange their views in person, there should be some kind of opportunity for them, like a group get-together once a week, and people who like to work at home can join by teleconferencing. So that's my view on this topic.

### German version

Das Wort „Homeoffice“ besteht aus zwei Begriffen, die bei mir ganz unterschiedliche Gefühle auslösen. Das Zuhause ist ein Ort, an dem man mit seiner Familie lebt, sich sicher und geborgen fühlt und seine Freizeit genießt. Das Büro hingegen ist ein Ort, an dem man seinen Lebensunterhalt verdient, seine Fähigkeiten einsetzt und sich auf seine Aufgaben konzentriert.

Heute werden diese beiden Begriffe kombiniert, und zwar vor allem aufgrund der Corona-Pandemie. Damals konnten die Menschen nicht mehr zur Arbeit fahren und waren gezwungen, von zu Hause aus zu arbeiten.

Das Homeoffice hat sowohl Vorteile als auch Nachteile. Einerseits ist man flexibler und spart sich den Arbeitsweg. Für Menschen, die gern ungestört in ihrer eigenen Blase arbeiten, ist das ideal. Andererseits kann es für diejenigen, die sich lieber persönlich mit Kollegen oder dem Chef austauschen, ziemlich belastend sein. Hinzu kommt, dass sich die Bedeutung des Zuhauses verändert: Es ist nicht mehr derselbe Ort wie früher.

Ein weiterer Nachteil betrifft die Produktivität. Manche Studien zeigen, dass das Homeoffice die Produktivität nicht unbedingt steigert. Deshalb braucht man meines Erachtens ein Gleichgewicht zwischen Flexibilität und Produktivität.

Aus Sicht des Arbeitgebers lässt sich viel Geld sparen, denn teure Büroräume in der Stadt sind nicht mehr nötig. Allerdings eignet sich das Homeoffice nur für Tätigkeiten, die am Computer erledigt werden können, und nicht etwa für den direkten Kundenkontakt. Außerdem sollte der Arbeitgeber die langfristigen Ziele im Blick behalten.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass man Produktivität und Flexibilität in Einklang bringen sollte. IT-Probleme sollten schnell gelöst werden, und für alle, die den persönlichen Austausch schätzen, könnte es einmal pro Woche ein Teamtreffen geben, an dem die anderen per Videokonferenz teilnehmen.

### Notes

- "Many studies conclude" was softened to „Manche Studien zeigen“, because research on home-office productivity is mixed.
- Reusable solution: a weekly in-person meetup, others join by video call.

## 3. Digitale Medien

**Prompt:** How digital media affect everyday life.

### English original

The word digital media breaks in me two separate sets of feelings. One is digital, and the other one is media. Digital is a fairly new phenomenon that probably started spreading into general society in the late 80s, with the incoming of computers, and later it led to mobile phones, then smartphones, the internet, and then today's modern world where we practically rely on everything digitally. Now, media has a long standing, even with the birth of human beings. It's a way of communication, a way of exchanging views among humans. So there is really a generational gap when we speak about the word media. People who were born before the 80s would treat media differently than the youngsters that you see today.

Now, digital media combines these two together. With the advent of the internet, you can get information fairly quickly and you can access knowledge, news, and all sorts of things. You can express your opinions freely, even without a face or a name. You can express your views to a much larger audience, and they could read and react to your opinions from anywhere in the world, and the language barrier is also demolished, and so on and so forth.

But there are many disadvantages to this digital way of living or media consumption. There is a tendency among people to believe everything that is in the digital ecosystem. And there are several studies that indicate that people will generally believe what is written more than what is spoken to them by a real person. Let's say a real person writes something digitally, and the same person says the same thing personally; the person at the receiving end will most certainly believe the things that are written digitally. This leads to the plethora of fake news that we see today. People use fake news against communities, against certain viewpoints, to get their ends going. In terms of advertisements, there is literally zero regulation on who can consume such media. In earlier times, when children were viewing television, there were certain programs that were highly regulated, that could not be viewed by children; there were warnings. But in today's world, even a child with a mobile phone can access anything. Even under the European Union's digital protection laws, they will ask, "Are you over 18?" You can just click yes, and then you can access all digital media. And this is really harmful for our children. They will consume violent and other behaviors. For example, in gaming there are certain vulgar phrases used, children get depressed, or there's cyberbullying and so on. So those are the disadvantages.

The advantages are that you can access almost any kind of knowledge at your fingertips. You can learn from official sources. You can access books digitally that at one time were not available. I remember when I was in my childhood, I craved a chess book. It wasn't available in any printed form. But now, if I was born today, I could read any amount of chess books; I can buy them online. Any kind of information you can get, you can expand your horizons. You can learn any skills that you want. You can learn any language. You can gather any knowledge. Everything is possible.

But children or youngsters, or people who are coming up in this digital age, must be properly educated about the disadvantages of this media, and it should be properly balanced. The government is responsible for making regulations, and I strongly believe that the regulations today aren't quite good. I would hope for certain social media like Facebook or WhatsApp there should be a worldwide regulation. For example, in the European Union, the things that we send on WhatsApp cannot be used for training, but in some third world countries this is not the case. And Facebook, because they are giving it for free, uses the information of people who post as data to train their AI models, which can in future lead to exploitation of certain groups. So we need a balance, we need reforms, we need concrete laws regarding social media consumption. At the same time, we must all embrace the possibilities that it opens up in our daily life.

### German version

Der Begriff „digitale Medien“ weckt bei mir zwei unterschiedliche Assoziationen. Das Digitale ist ein relativ neues Phänomen. Es begann etwa in den späten Achtzigern mit dem Computer, später kamen das Internet und das Smartphone hinzu, und heute verlassen wir uns praktisch in allen Bereichen auf digitale Technik. Medien hingegen gibt es, seit es Menschen gibt, denn sie dienen der Kommunikation und dem Austausch von Meinungen. Dabei gibt es einen deutlichen Generationsunterschied: Wer vor den Achtzigern geboren ist, geht ganz anders mit Medien um als die Jugendlichen von heute.

Die digitalen Medien verbinden beides. Einerseits kann man schnell auf Wissen und Nachrichten zugreifen und seine Meinung frei äußern, sogar anonym und vor einem weltweiten Publikum. Auch Sprachbarrieren spielen kaum noch eine Rolle.

Andererseits gibt es erhebliche Nachteile. Viele Menschen neigen dazu, alles zu glauben, was sie online lesen. Das begünstigt die Verbreitung von Fake News, die gezielt gegen bestimmte Gruppen eingesetzt werden. Hinzu kommt, dass Kinder kaum geschützt sind. Früher war das Fernsehprogramm für Kinder streng reguliert, heute genügt ein Klick auf „Ich bin über 18“, und schon hat ein Kind Zugang zu allen Inhalten. Gewalt, vulgäre Sprache in Online-Spielen und Cybermobbing können ihnen ernsthaft schaden.

Trotzdem überwiegen für mich die Chancen. Als Kind habe ich mir sehnlich ein Schachbuch gewünscht, aber es war nirgends erhältlich. Heute könnte ich online so viele Schachbücher kaufen, wie ich möchte. Man kann jede Fähigkeit und jede Sprache lernen und seinen Horizont erweitern.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass wir ein Gleichgewicht brauchen. Junge Menschen müssen über die Risiken aufgeklärt werden, und meines Erachtens ist der Staat dafür verantwortlich, strengere Gesetze zu erlassen, etwa zur Nutzung persönlicher Daten durch soziale Netzwerke für das Training von KI. Gleichzeitig sollten wir die Möglichkeiten, die sich uns eröffnen, im Alltag nutzen.

### Notes

- Ran over four minutes, so the German version is slightly tightened.
- Dropped the claim that people trust writing more than speech (doubtful), and kept the WhatsApp/Facebook details general, since they are hard to defend.
- The chess-book story is an excellent personal example; keep it.

## 4. Gesund leben

**Prompt:** How to stay healthy in everyday life.

### English original

The words healthy living bring in me a certain feeling of being free from bodily diseases and having sound mental health. This could also mean living long; for example, people who live to 90 plus or a hundred are usually labelled as people who lived healthily. Their lifestyle could be healthy so that they could live 90 plus. So this word could mean the overall health of an individual at present, and also the overall healthy way of living that the individual conducts during their lifetime, free from diseases and with good mental health.

Now, being healthy doesn't always mean being free from diseases. A person can be free from diseases but suffer in their mental health. So eating good food alone cannot be termed as healthy. Having good socio-economic well-being can also mean good health.

Being health conscious has several advantages. One is obviously that you can live longer and more productively. If the citizens of a country as a whole are healthy, the productivity of the country increases, and there's less spending on healthcare, and the country can divert those funds to, for example, education. But getting access to gyms or good food is not always possible, and that is one of the policy shifts that we need to think of.

And one of the disadvantages of healthy living: people would ask, healthy living is universally accepted, what would be the disadvantages? For example, living healthily while at the same time destroying the environment is not healthy living. In my opinion, it's not sustainable. So being sustainable and living happily and healthily is an important topic. For example, if you are taking nutrient supplements, have you ever thought about where these supplements come from? They come from, for example, fish liver oil. You're killing a lot of fish, and there are major corporations working in these fields, polluting the environment. If you have an air conditioner at home that is healthy for you, it is giving out harmful gases outside. So being healthy doesn't mean that you can live without thinking about others. Being healthy and sustainable is something we need to be aware of, and being healthy but not sustainable is one of the disadvantages of this healthy living.

So we all need to exercise, we all need to live healthily, we all need to live happily with our family and friends and society, but we also need to think about nature and the other animals around us. So the government needs to make policies to give citizens access to exercise, gyms, and good healthy food, by making sure that the things they buy from supermarkets are harmless, and also they should regulate companies and ensure that healthy living is not at the cost of sustainable living.

### German version

Wenn ich an ein gesundes Leben denke, verbinde ich damit vor allem zwei Dinge: frei von körperlichen Krankheiten zu sein und eine stabile psychische Gesundheit zu haben. Oft bezeichnet man auch Menschen, die über 90 Jahre alt werden, als gesund, weil sie ihr Leben lang gesund gelebt haben. Der Begriff beschreibt also sowohl den aktuellen Gesundheitszustand als auch die Lebensweise eines Menschen.

Allerdings bedeutet Gesundheit nicht nur, keine Krankheiten zu haben. Man kann körperlich gesund sein und trotzdem psychisch leiden. Deshalb reicht gesunde Ernährung allein nicht aus, auch das soziale und wirtschaftliche Wohlbefinden spielt eine wichtige Rolle.

Ein gesundheitsbewusstes Leben hat zahlreiche Vorteile. Man lebt nicht nur länger, sondern auch produktiver. Wenn die Bürger eines Landes insgesamt gesund sind, steigt die Produktivität und die Gesundheitsausgaben sinken. Das eingesparte Geld ließe sich zum Beispiel in die Bildung investieren. Allerdings hat nicht jeder Zugang zu Fitnessstudios oder gesunden Lebensmitteln.

Man könnte fragen, welche Nachteile ein gesundes Leben überhaupt haben soll. Meines Erachtens ist Gesundheit auf Kosten der Umwelt nicht nachhaltig. Wer zum Beispiel Nahrungsergänzungsmittel wie Fischöl nimmt, denkt selten darüber nach, wie viele Fische dafür sterben und wie stark die Herstellung die Umwelt belastet. Auch eine Klimaanlage sorgt zwar für ein angenehmes Raumklima, gibt aber schädliche Gase ab.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass wir uns bewegen, gesund leben und unsere Beziehungen pflegen sollten, ohne dabei die Natur und die Tiere zu vergessen. Der Staat sollte allen Bürgern den Zugang zu Sport und gesunden Lebensmitteln ermöglichen, die Unternehmen regulieren und sicherstellen, dass gesundes Leben nicht auf Kosten der Nachhaltigkeit geht.

### Notes

- The "healthy but not sustainable" disadvantage is original, but drifts off topic. Keep it short and also mention the obvious disadvantages (cost, time).

## 5. Wohnen in der Stadt oder auf dem Land

**Prompt:** Living in the city versus the countryside.

### English original

The word Wohnen, or living, brings in me a certain feeling of hope. It may not be the case for everyone. But living in the sense that we are doing our duties, we are living happily and healthily, and we are enjoying life.

Now, we can think of two different ways of modern living: a person living in a city, or a person living in a village or countryside. Both have their own advantages and disadvantages. For example, a person is bound to city life when he or she has a job there, has a family there, and there is no way for them to live in the countryside unless, for example, they can have a home office there. Usually in today's world, living in a city means that you ought to have a job in or around the city. Even when that is the case, people get really not so satisfied with city living, and they retire early and go to a village. So we need to think about these two separate scenarios.

The advantages of city life are that you get better facilities, access to jobs and resources, and there is culture, there are ways of enjoyment, ways of expression; you can meet a lot of people of your own mental texture, you can exchange your views, and there are a lot of opportunities in general. In the case of the countryside, the advantages are that you are free from the polluting and stressful life that the city entails. In the countryside, life is slow, relaxed, stress-free. But at the same time it has certain disadvantages: there is less access to doctors, facilities, or internet. Even a person who doesn't like city life cannot just one day go to the countryside, even if they have a home office possibility, because the facilities are just not there. There could be no good internet connection, and you need to spend time travelling long distances to buy your groceries, for example. So saving enough time is not really a possibility in the countryside. But there are people who ought to live in the countryside, like farmers who feed millions of city dwellers. They ought to live on the farmlands, and it is their way of living.

So when I separate the way of living and where you want to live, I would categorize people into two categories. Some people would like an easygoing way of living; for them it is advantageous to live in the countryside. But what would they do with their life, what would they do for the benefit of the community, if they don't like farming, for example? So there is always this conflict going on between the city and the countryside.

The one thing we can do is include the good of both ends. In the city, we can install certain places where people who like to explore nature can do so inside the city. Even in New York City there's a big forest area where people can go and camp during their free time. And this city, Hamburg, can literally be called a tree city. You can see trees everywhere. For example, on the Altmarsch side, the swampy side, there are forests, churches surrounded by forest. So the distinction between city life and stress-free countryside life is getting smaller and smaller in cities like Hamburg, and this is what the authorities should try to instill. Children growing up must explore nature and not be around a polluted community all the time; there should be possibilities for them inside the cities. And for all people there should be a place to relax; we can take the good part of the countryside and instill it in cities, so that the distinction between the countryside and the cities is gone, and we have complete wholesome living in a city. At the same time, we can do the same in the countryside, where we could provide better facilities, good access to internet, and even if you don't have physical doctors around you, there is the possibility of telemedicine. With the advent of AI, it is possible to have city-like facilities there. So taking the good from both cities and countryside will inevitably lead to good living. The government should make policies to enforce the good from both scenarios, in both cities and villages.

### German version

Das Wort „Wohnen“ weckt bei mir ein Gefühl der Hoffnung. Für mich bedeutet es, seinen Pflichten nachzugehen, glücklich und gesund zu leben und das Leben zu genießen.

Heutzutage gibt es zwei Lebensformen: das Leben in der Stadt und das Leben auf dem Land. Wer in der Stadt wohnt, ist meistens an seinen Arbeitsplatz und seine Familie gebunden. Trotzdem sind viele Menschen mit dem Stadtleben unzufrieden und ziehen nach der Rente aufs Land.

Einerseits bietet die Stadt viele Vorteile: bessere Infrastruktur, mehr Arbeitsplätze, ein vielfältiges kulturelles Angebot und die Möglichkeit, Gleichgesinnte zu treffen. Andererseits ist das Leben auf dem Land ruhiger, entspannter und frei von Lärm und Luftverschmutzung. Allerdings gibt es dort weniger Ärzte, oft kein gutes Internet, und für den Einkauf muss man weite Strecken fahren. Deshalb kann man nicht einfach aufs Land ziehen, selbst wenn man im Homeoffice arbeiten könnte. Hinzu kommt, dass manche Menschen auf dem Land leben müssen, zum Beispiel Landwirte, die Millionen von Stadtbewohnern ernähren.

Meines Erachtens liegt die Lösung darin, das Beste aus beiden Welten zu verbinden. In Hamburg sieht man zum Beispiel überall Bäume, und in den Marschlanden findet man sogar Kirchen, die von Wäldern umgeben sind. In solchen Städten wird der Unterschied zwischen Stadt und Land immer kleiner. Vor allem Kinder sollten auch in der Stadt die Möglichkeit haben, die Natur zu erleben. Umgekehrt sollte man auf dem Land die Infrastruktur verbessern, etwa durch schnelles Internet und Telemedizin.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass der Staat dafür sorgen sollte, die Vorteile beider Lebensformen sowohl in der Stadt als auch auf dem Land zu verwirklichen.

### Notes

- Well over four minutes; the middle wandered. The German version is condensed.
- Pick one strong example (Hamburg) and drop the second (New York).

## 6. Online-Lernen oder Präsenzunterricht

**Prompt:** Online learning versus classroom learning (theme: Bildung).

### English original

Online versus classroom learning. If you take apart online and classroom, we'll have the word learning. What this word learning entails is that a person tries to imbibe knowledge from a source so that they can enhance their quality of life, their society, and in a broader sense their country or the world.

Now, we have two themes here, online and classroom learning. If this question were asked to a person living in the 80s, there would be no possibility of getting an answer about online learning, because it didn't exist at that time. So this is a fairly new concept. It came in the late 80s with the advent of the internet, that people can learn things not from books or from classrooms, but using the internet and a virtual classroom.

There are several advantages of classroom learning and online learning. Let's look at online learning. The students, or the person who is learning, are more flexible. There are more possibilities of flexibility. They can sit anywhere in the world and learn anything at all. There is no rigid structure to it. And they can even work on their learning while they are working, while they are in an Arbeit. So there are many possibilities opening up in terms of getting the knowledge they want to improve their lifestyle and so on.

One disadvantage is that you miss the exchange with the students and the teachers. For example, if you make a mistake or you ask a question, the teacher can directly respond and look at your personality, and there is a sort of rapport building along the years with a personal kind of exchange. This is what I feel is being lost in online learning, where teachers are to some extent detached from the psychological part of this teaching-learning process. And there is no real exchange between the students. Students express a lot of views about everything, and this can be seen commonly happening in classroom settings, which is really not happening in online learning.

But we can try to bring the best of both worlds together. We should make sure that students are properly taught in online learning, given proper attention, with certain time periods for them to exchange with the students and with the teachers, or even bringing everybody together once a week in a place where they can exchange views and see each other in person. I think we can bring both worlds together by making use of the vast advantages of online learning, which is obviously the future, so that we can have a better community of learners and teachers working together. For this there should be concrete policies by the government regulating online classrooms, providing support for students to travel or to meet up, and providing proper guidance and proper training to teachers, so that this essential part is not missed: that the students and teachers, and the students themselves, must exchange their views.

### German version

Wenn man die beiden Begriffe betrachtet, bleibt ein gemeinsames Wort übrig: Lernen. Lernen bedeutet für mich, Wissen aufzunehmen, um die eigene Lebensqualität, die Gesellschaft und im weiteren Sinne das Land oder sogar die Welt zu verbessern.

Hätte man diese Frage jemandem in den Achtzigerjahren gestellt, hätte er über Online-Lernen gar nichts sagen können, denn es existierte damals noch nicht. Erst mit dem Internet wurde es möglich, nicht nur aus Büchern oder im Klassenzimmer zu lernen, sondern auch in einem virtuellen Klassenraum.

Das Online-Lernen hat zahlreiche Vorteile. Die Lernenden sind flexibler, können von überall auf der Welt fast alles lernen und sind nicht an einen starren Stundenplan gebunden. Außerdem lässt sich das Lernen problemlos mit der Arbeit verbinden.

Allerdings fehlt dabei der persönliche Austausch mit den Lehrkräften und den anderen Lernenden. Im Präsenzunterricht kann die Lehrkraft direkt auf Fehler oder Fragen eingehen und die Persönlichkeit der Schüler wahrnehmen. Mit der Zeit entsteht so eine echte Beziehung. Im Online-Unterricht hingegen sind die Lehrkräfte vom psychologischen Teil des Lernprozesses weitgehend abgekoppelt. Hinzu kommt, dass auch der lebendige Austausch zwischen den Lernenden, der im Klassenzimmer ganz selbstverständlich ist, kaum stattfindet.

Meines Erachtens sollten wir das Beste aus beiden Welten verbinden. Online-Lernende sollten genug Aufmerksamkeit bekommen, feste Zeiten für den Austausch mit Lehrkräften haben und sich zum Beispiel einmal pro Woche persönlich treffen.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass das Online-Lernen zweifellos die Zukunft ist. Damit dabei der persönliche Austausch nicht verloren geht, sollte der Staat klare Regeln für Online-Unterricht schaffen, Lehrkräfte gezielt schulen und Lernende bei Präsenztreffen unterstützen.

### Notes

- Online learning really took off in the 1990s–2000s, not the late 80s, so the dates are kept vague.
- One of the tightest speeches. The weekly in-person meetup reappears (as in Homeoffice).

## 7. Tourismus: Fluch oder Segen?

**Prompt:** Does tourism do more good or harm?

### English original

Whether tourism does more good or harm. So let us look at the word tourism. Why would someone travel to another place, far away from their homeland, which is really the meaning of tourism? One idea is that they would like to expand their horizons; this is something that I hear most often. Some people like to learn about other cultures, how other people live, and to experience different cuisines. And just plainly a relaxing holiday. So people travel for all sorts of reasons, and these three things come to my mind as the main reasons for travelling for leisure.

Now, tourism, for example in Venice or in Barcelona, can have catastrophic effects on the local community. We have seen locals taking to the streets with placards, asking for tourists to be banned from their areas. Why? Because tourists make rent prices higher. For example, there is a certain group of people called digital nomads; they use online work and just stay in the tourist area so they can have the best of both worlds. But then the rents of the cities rise. And the plethora of tourists coming into the city makes local living really difficult. For example, a local person with their family cannot enjoy a stress-free holiday in their own city, because there are a huge number of tourists polluting, and all sorts of things like noise pollution and so on.

An advantage is fairly obvious: the city gets a lot of money from the tourists. Commerce booms, and they are taxed, and the government also gets a part. So we cannot really ban tourists, but we must, for example, put some sort of regulation on tourism.

For example, there are quotas. Let's look at a different way of tourism, religious tourism. For example, Saudi Arabia puts quotas on certain countries, so that a large number of people are not allowed once the quota is filled. They have a certain sense that we can hold this many people, so we give this many people places this year, so that everybody's happy: the local shops are happy that they get enough tourists, and the local people are happy that they can enjoy their lives stress-free.

Now there's also the aspect of pollution by tourism. Rich people take cruise ships or personal flights, and even in the summer holidays we can see from flight data that the number of flights is too high, which is causing real damage to the environment in terms of air pollution. So a way of tourism with no harm to the environment must be established. The government should try to put a carbon tax on the tourism-based travel agencies which promote cruise ships or personal jets, so that the highly damaging nature of tourism is discouraged.

But tourism is really something that enhances our view of the world. It must not be banned entirely, but we must have the best of both worlds, and the government must make some tight regulations, as I mentioned before.

### German version

Was bedeutet eigentlich Tourismus? Warum reist jemand weit weg von seiner Heimat? Mir fallen dafür vor allem drei Gründe ein: Man möchte seinen Horizont erweitern, andere Kulturen und Küchen kennenlernen oder sich einfach im Urlaub erholen.

Allerdings kann der Tourismus für die Einheimischen verheerende Folgen haben. In Venedig oder Barcelona gehen die Menschen bereits mit Plakaten auf die Straße und fordern, den Tourismus einzuschränken. Ein Grund dafür sind die steigenden Mieten, zu denen zum Beispiel auch digitale Nomaden beitragen, die online arbeiten und gleichzeitig in Touristenorten wohnen. Hinzu kommt, dass Einheimische ihre eigene Stadt wegen der Menschenmassen und des Lärms kaum noch genießen können.

Andererseits liegt der Vorteil auf der Hand: Der Tourismus bringt viel Geld in die Stadt, der Handel blüht, und der Staat profitiert von den Steuereinnahmen. Deshalb kann man Touristen nicht einfach verbieten, sondern sollte den Tourismus regulieren.

Ein Beispiel dafür sind Quoten. Saudi-Arabien legt etwa für die Pilgerreise nach Mekka eine Höchstzahl an Pilgern pro Land fest. So profitieren die örtlichen Geschäfte, und gleichzeitig können die Einheimischen ohne Stress leben.

Ein weiteres Problem ist die Umweltverschmutzung. Kreuzfahrten, Privatjets und die vielen Flüge in den Sommerferien schaden dem Klima erheblich. Meines Erachtens sollte der Staat deshalb eine CO₂-Steuer für Reiseveranstalter einführen, die solche Angebote bewerben.

Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass Tourismus unseren Blick auf die Welt erweitert und nicht ganz verboten werden sollte. Wir brauchen aber klare Regeln, damit Einheimische und Umwelt nicht darunter leiden.

### Notes

- The Saudi quota example is unusual and memorable; keep it brief, since religion can pull a discussion off course.

## Observed speaking pattern

All seven talks follow the same five-step line, which will become the skeleton for the frames.

1. **Unpack the word:** what the key term means or what feeling it gives me (all 7 talks).
2. **Background or cause:** history, origin or why the issue exists (e.g. COVID, the 80s, causes of decay).
3. **Advantages vs. disadvantages:** usually one side, then the other, often split by type of person.
4. **One concrete example:** protected areas, chess book, Hamburg trees, Saudi quotas.
5. **"Best of both worlds" conclusion:** a balance, with the government regulating, educating or supporting (all 7 talks).

**Recurring ideas worth turning into reusable phrases:** balance between two sides, the government must make policies, education and awareness, a weekly in-person meetup, sustainability.

**Habits to watch in the exam:**

- Talks often run over 4 minutes; the opening and the middle tend to wander.
- Claims like "many studies show" are risky; soften them or drop them.
- The direct question is sometimes not answered (Umweltschutz); state a clear position early.
- More than one example per point dilutes it; pick the strongest one.
